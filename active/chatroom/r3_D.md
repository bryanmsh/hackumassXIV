ON THE DUAL GATE:
A dual gate is realistic only if it is small and timeboxed. LectureLens's gate costs one API call and one hour. FocusPage's gate needs a working Gemini box to SAM to recolor pipeline, and Crosscheck's needs 15 degraded photos someone must stage. Running both in parallel burns half the team for the first 6 hours and fractures focus. My ruling is to commit to FocusPage up front and run only its 10-page gate, with a hard deadline at hour 4. Crosscheck is the pre-declared fallback, and its cheap glare-baseline check runs in the background by one person in under an hour. A real fallback decided in advance beats a symmetric gate that tries to decide everything.
