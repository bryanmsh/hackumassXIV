# Problem Validation

1. Who has this problem? Name a concrete persona.
2. What is the current workflow and pain?
3. Which step is improved by AI rather than a deterministic rule?
4. What public or synthetic data can demonstrate it safely?
5. What measurable baseline can be shown in a demo?
6. What would falsify the idea?

**Template**: For [user], [problem] causes [cost]. Our [workflow] changes [metric] from [baseline] to [result], measured on [test].
