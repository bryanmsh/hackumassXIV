# Hackachusetts 2026 Research

## Verified event overview
- Saturday October 24, 2026; 8 AM–8 PM ET, hacking 10 AM–8 PM; submissions closed 8 PM.
- Nine campuses, four tracks, advertised $30,000 prize pool, up to four people per team.
- Tracks: Biotech & Healthcare; Robotics & Hardware; Energy & Climate Tech; Civic Action.
- Official challenge portal provided *illustrative prompts* and downloadable starter kits; prompts were not mandatory project briefs.
- Judges reviewed submissions Saturday evening. Official page says ask campus hosts for scoring rubric; **do not invent a numeric rubric**.
- Official winners by track:
  - Healthcare: Pharma Pool, PipetteCoach, OncoPath.
  - Robotics: Fellowship, MayDay, Drones for the Visually Disabled.
  - Climate: FreshCast, Cool Boston, SwitchPoint.
  - Civic: Tea Party, Piper, Who Gets Missed.
- These names do not independently establish winning-project technical details or judging rationale.
