# Civic Action

Official illustrative themes: simplified public services, resilient communities, connected places, better college experience, pathways to education and opportunity. Source: https://hackachusetts2026.vercel.app/

## Candidate MVP: benefits navigator with citations
Input: user-selected, non-sensitive scenario. Output: shortlist of programs with official links, requirements, and uncertainty flags. Metric: grounded citation coverage on 10 test scenarios; time to locate source vs manual baseline.

## Alternative
Campus accessibility route planner; local resource discovery for students.
