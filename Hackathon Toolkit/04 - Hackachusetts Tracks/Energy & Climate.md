# Energy & Climate

Official illustrative themes: climate resilience, affordable buildings, clean-grid reliability, ocean protection, blue economy. Source: https://hackachusetts2026.vercel.app/

## Candidate MVP: building energy anomaly explainer
Input: open or synthetic hourly energy readings. Output: flagged anomalies, cost estimates under stated assumptions, actions ranked by expected savings. Metric: anomaly detection on labeled examples, estimated kWh vs baseline.

## Alternative
Flood preparedness visualization with public hazard layers and cited caveats.

## Data
- https://data.mass.gov/
- https://www.eia.gov/opendata/
- https://www.noaa.gov/

## Risk
Do not imply synthetic savings are verified field results; label forecasts and assumptions.
